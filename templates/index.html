Water Quality & Turbidity Anomaly Monitor

Optical nephelometry, particulate scattering, and biochemical oxygen demand indicators.

🧪 Water Quality Indicators Explained

Turbidity (NTU): Measures optical backscatter from suspended particulates. High values (> 10 NTU) in coastal reefs indicate river runoff, dredging, or potential industrial sediment discharge.

Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L): Critical indicator for aquatic respiration. Depletions below 5.0 mg/L signal hypoxia or high organic decomposition.

pH Balance: Baseline ocean pH is ~8.1. Ocean acidification or localized industrial effluents alter carbonic equilibria.

⚠️ Scientific Caution & Protocol

An elevated turbidity or DO spike is an observational indicator, NOT legal proof of pollution. High turbidity frequently occurs naturally from tidal scouring, high waves, or monsoon precipitation.

ORCA Mandate: Field physical sampling, satellite imagery cross-checks, and sensor wiper recalibrations are recommended before assigning liability or issuing public closures.

About ORCA – Marine Ecosystem Intelligence

                    

                        "ORCA – From Ocean Data to Informed Action."
                    

                    

                        

                            Marine ecosystems are undergoing unprecedented transformations due to plastic accumulation, oil and chemical runoffs, rising sea surface temperatures, ocean acidification, and localized hypoxia. Traditional monitoring workflows suffer from siloed datasets and slow manual interpretation.
                        

                        

                            ORCA bridges this gap by unifying oceanographic telemetry, satellite observations, and marine biodiversity logs into an intelligent, collaborative multi-agent architecture.

🛡️ Safety, Reliability & Ethics Disclaimer

                        

                            

                                Human-in-the-Loop Imperative: ORCA is strictly engineered to augment and support human experts—marine researchers, port authorities, and environmental scientists—not replace them.
                            

                            

                                Illustrative Sample Data: The observations featured in this prototype are clearly labelled sample or historical datasets. Unusual sensor readings warrant rapid scientific validation, not definitive assumptions of contamination.
